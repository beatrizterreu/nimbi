How can we help?

Find answers below, or reach out directly.

Getting started
How do I create my first story?
When you first open Nimbi, you'll set up a child profile: name, age, and favorite interests. Once that's done, you'll land on the Home screen where you can pick a story length, choose a mood, and tap Generate Story. Your first story will be ready in a few seconds.
Can I add more than one child?
Yes! You can add multiple child profiles from the Profiles section (bookmark icon on the Home screen). Each child has their own interests and story history, so bedtime stays personal for everyone.
Do I need to create an account?
No. Nimbi works without any account or login. Everything is saved locally on your device. We believe bedtime shouldn't require signing up for anything.
What age range is Nimbi designed for?
Nimbi is best suited for children aged 2 to 10. Stories automatically adjust in vocabulary, length, and complexity based on your child's age. Toddler stories are shorter and simpler; stories for older children may have richer language and more developed plots.
Stories & content
How are the stories generated?
Each story is created by an AI model at the moment you tap Generate. It uses the details from your child's profile: their interests, age, and mood, to craft a unique story. No two stories are the same. An internet connection is required for generation, which typically takes 5 to 15 seconds.
Are the stories safe for children?
Yes. Nimbi's story generation is specifically constrained to produce calm, age-appropriate content. Stories never include violence, scary elements, or overstimulating plots. The AI is instructed to end every story peacefully, helping children wind down, not stay awake.
What are the "learning values" I see on each story?
Each story weaves in a gentle learning value, such as kindness, courage, or curiosity, naturally into the narrative. It's never preachy or lesson-like; it's just part of the story world. The value label appears as a small pill above the story title.
Can I save stories to read again?
Yes. Tap the heart icon while reading to save a story to your Favorites. Saved stories are available offline and remain on your device until you delete them.
Language
Which languages does Nimbi support?
Stories can be generated in English, Spanish, French, Italian, German, and Portuguese. The app interface is available in English and Spanish. Both settings are independent, so you can use the app in one language while generating stories in another.
How do I change the app language?
Go to the Settings screen (gear icon on the Home screen) and select your preferred language under App Language. The change takes effect immediately.
How do I change the story language?
On the Home screen, use the language toggle to choose which language your next story will be generated in. This only affects the story content, not the app interface.
Privacy & data
Where is my data stored?
All your data: child profiles, saved stories, and settings, is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. The only data that leaves your device is the story generation request, which includes your child's age, interests, and mood. This is sent to our AI provider to generate the story and is not retained.
Does Nimbi track my usage?
Nimbi collects minimal, anonymized analytics to understand how the app is used. This data is never tied to identifiable information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
How do I delete my data?
Since all data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing the app. All profiles, saved stories, and preferences will be permanently deleted. We do not hold a copy.
Technical
The story is taking a long time to generate. What's wrong?
Story generation requires an active internet connection and typically takes 5 to 15 seconds. If it's taking longer, check your connection. If the issue persists, close and reopen the app and try again.
Can I use Nimbi offline?
You can read saved (favorited) stories offline anytime. Generating new stories requires an internet connection since the AI runs in the cloud.
Which devices does Nimbi support?
Nimbi is currently available for iPhone and iPad running iOS 16 or later. We're working on an Android version, stay tuned.
