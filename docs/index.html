Why Nimbi

Designed for the end of the day

Every detail (colors, pacing, content) is chosen to calm rather than stimulate.

Made for your child

Stories adapt to your child's name, age, favorite topics, and tonight's mood. No two stories are the same.

Calm by design

Slow pacing, gentle language, no cliffhangers. Every story winds down naturally, just like a good bedtime should.

Private & safe

Stories are saved on your device. No account needed, no data sold. Nimbi is a tool for families, not a platform.

Multiple languages

Generate stories in English, Spanish, French, Italian, German and Portuguese. Every night in the language you prefer.

How it works

A story in three steps

1

Set up a profile

Enter your child's name, age, and the topics they love: animals, space, magic, and more.

2

Choose tonight's vibe

Pick a story length, mood, and an optional theme. Nimbi takes it from there.

3

Read & drift off

Your story appears in seconds. Read it aloud or let the soft screen guide you both.
